Hello! I am Bo Chen. I am currently a Privacy Engineer at ByteDance. Prior to joining ByteDance, I earned a Ph.D. degree, majored in Mechanical Engineering at the University of Florida. I was under the supervision of Dr. Matthew Hale and was a member of [the Control, Optimization, and Robotics Engineering (CORE) Lab](https://corelab.ece.gatech.edu/). After the graduation of my Ph.D., I worked as a Postdoc at Georgia Institute of Technology and a data scientist at Geotab.

**Research Interests:**
I am interested in developing novel privacy-enhanced tools to facilitate the sharing and analysis of sensitive data. My focus lies particularly in privatizing data generated by control-related systems, such as path planning systems and multiagent control systems, with a strong mathematical protection of differential privacy. 

If you’re interested in learning more, click [here](../_pages/publications.md) for a list of my recent projects.
